Ghost in the Machine

Witching Hour

A real house kept a log of every room, every 10 minutes, for four months. An open tabular model learned from each fortnight what the house should feel like at night. When a room drifted outside that range, something was up.

Pick a night. The Medium tells you what haunted it. The Skeptic tells you what probably happened. The Morning note tells a family what they would want to know if someone they love lived there alone. None of them is allowed a sentence without a sensor to back it.

The nights

Each square is one night, midnight to 6 AM. The brighter it glows, the further a reading strayed from what the house expected.

Ask the house

An agent with four tools: rank the nights, scan one night, ask TabPFN what it expected at a given minute, and call a narrator. Gemma decides which tool to use.

Did TabPFN earn its place?

I planted fake ghosts (smooth bumps of known size and time) into real nights and checked who noticed them, at a 5% false alarm rate.
