Witching Hour

A ghost-hunting kit for real houses. An open tabular model, the spirit meter, learns what every room feels like when nothing is haunting it. When a reading breaks that pattern in the dark, the needle jumps and the crew takes the case. None of them may say a word the sensors cannot back.

🗺️The haunting map

Every square is one night of a real house, midnight to 6 AM. The brighter it glows, the further a room strayed from what the spirit meter expected. A blue dot means a room stayed below 18 °C.

🧰What is in the kit

  • The spirit meterTabPFN, an open tabular foundation model, reads the house's last 14 days in context and predicts a range for every room, every 10 minutes. Readings far outside it are apparitions.
  • The crewLuna, Dr. Bigotes and Xochi are Gemma 4 running on your own machine through Ollama, each with a personality and a job.
  • Cite or vanishEvery sentence must cite an apparition or a cold-room check, and every number must match. Anything else is dropped before you read it.
  • The watch never sleepsEach night is a durable Temporal workflow. Kill the worker or the model mid-night and it picks up where it stopped.
  • VoicesThe crew reads aloud with ElevenLabs. Only verified sentences are spoken.
  • Private by designA house's night logs say when people sleep and when the house is empty. Everything runs at home; this public demo only replays results.
